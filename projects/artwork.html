Digital introspection is an interaction design project exploring new ways of contextualizing screen usage, disconnecting, and preserve introspective states such as mind-wandering and daydreaming.

The project consists of three main scenarios, each exploring a theme relating to digital wellbeing, displaying a breadth of possibilities and new opportunities for how we define our relationship to our devices.
